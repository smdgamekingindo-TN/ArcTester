ArcTester Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-27-v3 · Published draft

Publication blocker: Final legal review, verified provider processing locations, and production retention periods must be completed before this notice is treated as final. Do not rely on this draft as a legal compliance certification.

1. Who operates ArcTester?

ArcTester is operated by Shaik Mahammad Hussain remotely from India. There is no public business premises or published residential address. Privacy and grievance contact: smdgameking@gmail.com (email correspondence).

2. Personal data we process

Depending on your account and features, ArcTester may process:

  • Account information: email address, full name, password hash and account approval/status.
  • Legal acceptance evidence: Terms and Privacy Notice versions, server-recorded acceptance time, registration channel and adult age attestation.
  • Academic information: college or polytechnic name/code, diploma branch/code and registration number; postal PIN only if provided.
  • Learning and assessment data: questions you create, answers, exam/practice submissions, scores, progress and learning analytics.
  • Security information: login and session metadata, device details, passkey public credentials, encrypted authenticator secrets, hashed recovery codes and security audit events.
  • Support or privacy request information that you choose to send.

ArcTester should not store readable passwords. Never send passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or session tokens in support messages.

3. Purposes and lawful processing

We use data to create and secure accounts; verify academic context and account eligibility; provide lessons, practice exams and assigned assessments; calculate scores and progress; prevent abuse; investigate security events; maintain backups and service reliability; and respond to account, privacy and grievance requests. Data should be limited to what is necessary for each purpose. Any optional or new purpose must be separately explained and, where required, separately consented to.

4. AI and analytics

Student-identifying information must not be included in model-training datasets by default. If a future feature proposes sending personal data to an AI or analytics provider, the operator must review necessity, disclose the provider and purpose, apply minimisation and safeguards, and obtain any required consent before enabling it.

5. Sharing and service providers

Data may be processed by authorized staff and contracted providers needed to host the web application/API, database, backups, file storage, email delivery and security services. The operator must publish the actual providers, processing purposes, locations and contractual safeguards here before final publication: Current providers: Vercel (web hosting/deployment), Render (API and PostgreSQL hosting), and Google (email service). Exact production and backup processing regions have not yet been independently verified and will be published after confirmation.. We do not claim a specific country of storage until production and backup locations are verified.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described, account operation, academic records, security and applicable legal obligations. Current engineering documentation describes 365-day audit-event retention and pruning of certain expired session/login metadata; the operator must verify production settings and publish category-specific periods before final publication. Backups may expire on a separate rotation schedule: Backup expiry window is not yet verified; do not assume immediate removal from backups.. Deletion from live systems may not immediately remove immutable backup copies.

7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to information about your personal data, correction or updating, erasure, withdrawal of consent, and raise a grievance. Use the verified contact above. We will verify identity, assess applicable retention/legal constraints, and respond through the published process. Withdrawal of consent should be as easy as giving it; it may affect features that need the data.

8. Security

ArcTester uses safeguards such as password hashing, role-based access checks, session revocation, MFA/passkeys where enabled, scoped integration keys, rate limiting, encrypted authenticator secrets and security logging. Access should follow least privilege and be reviewed. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Suspected incidents are assessed and notified as required by applicable law and contracts.

9. Student eligibility

ArcTester is intended for students, including diploma students under 18. Students under 18 may register; ArcTester does not require a parent/guardian account. Users are responsible for their account activity and providing accurate information. The project operator remains responsible for personal-data processing and safeguards required by applicable law; users cannot waive or assume the operator's legal obligations.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this notice when data practices materially change, record the version/effective date, and provide notice of material changes. Privacy and grievance contact: smdgameking@gmail.com. The operator can be contacted by email; no public office address is maintained.
