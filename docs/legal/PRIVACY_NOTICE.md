## 2. Data processed

Depending on features used, ArcTester may process:

- Account email, name, password hash, account status and registration timestamps.
- Legal acceptance evidence: Terms/Privacy Notice versions, server-recorded acceptance time, registration source and adult age attestation.
- Academic profile: institution name/code, branch/code and diploma registration number; postal PIN only if supplied.
- Learning data: created questions, practice and exam answers, submissions, scores, progress and learning analytics.
- Security data: session/device metadata, login history, passkey public credentials, encrypted authenticator secrets, hashed recovery codes and security audit events.
- Support or privacy-request details voluntarily submitted by the user.

ArcTester must not store readable passwords or include student-identifying data in model-training datasets by default.

## 3. Purposes

Data is used to create and secure accounts; verify academic context and eligibility; deliver lessons, practice exams and assigned assessments; score work and show progress; prevent abuse; investigate incidents; maintain reliability and backups; and respond to account, privacy and grievance requests. Data should be limited to what is necessary for each purpose. New or optional purposes require a separate review and notice/consent where applicable.

## 4. AI and analytics

Student-identifying information is excluded from model-training datasets by default. Any future processing of personal data by an AI or analytics provider requires purpose review, minimisation, provider disclosure, contractual/security review and any required separate consent before activation.

## 6. Retention

Data is retained only as needed for the purposes above, account operation, academic records, security and applicable law. Current engineering documentation describes 365-day audit-event retention and pruning of certain expired session/login metadata; production configuration must be verified. Publish category-specific retention for account, academic, consent-evidence and backup records: **[VERIFIED RETENTION SCHEDULE REQUIRED]**. Deletion from live systems may not immediately remove immutable backup copies.

## 7. Rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to information about their personal data, correction/updating, erasure, withdrawal of consent and grievance redressal. Use the verified contact above. Requests will be identity-checked and assessed against applicable legal/academic retention requirements. Withdrawal of consent should be as easy as giving it and may disable features that need the data.

## 8. Security

ArcTester uses safeguards including password hashing, server-side role checks, session revocation, MFA/passkeys where enabled, scoped integration keys, rate limiting, encrypted authenticator secrets and security logging. Access should follow least privilege. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Incidents are assessed and notified as required by applicable law and contract.

## 9. Children and age eligibility

ArcTester registration currently requires an adult age attestation. Students under 18 must not register until a verified parent/guardian consent workflow and applicable child-data safeguards are implemented and reviewed. A checkbox or a student's self-attestation is not verifiable parental consent. The adult attestation is a gating control, not proof of age or a compliance certification.
